How to play
  • Start mining to generate HashDust over time.
  • Sell HashDust to earn Cash for upgrades.
  • Upgrade CPU/GPU to increase H/s.
  • Reforge to gain permanent Blueprint bonuses.
  • Hold Overclock to build a temporary multiplier.
Controls
  • Press P to toggle the pause menu.
  • Use Pause to Restart or End a run.
